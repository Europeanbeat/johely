Review · balatonjohelyindex.hu · 2026-09-14

Is the Jó Hely Index site ready for the public and for the tourism board?

Seven independent reviews: four checked the site line by line against the 168-page study (front matter and method, results, conclusions and program, handbook), one read it as a journalist and average reader, one as a national tourism-agency analyst, one rendered it on desktop and mobile. This page merges them.

Short answer. For the public and the media: yes, after half a day of edits. The writing is plain, the structure is right, and every number checked matches the study. But the headline says something the study explicitly forbids, five footer links go nowhere, and a placeholder is still visible. For the national tourism board: not yet. The site is a well-told story, not an evidence product. The board's agenda, the data behind it, and who submits it are only in the PDF, which the site doesn't link.

Average reader3/5

Plain language, findings before method, jargon avoided. Loses points for the "Balaton is a good place" verdict and for numbers that exist only inside charts.

Journalists3/5

Quotable, citation licence present. No publication date, no named contact, no data files, and the site hands them the wrong headline.

National tourism board2/5

Gets the 41 / 32 / 27 competence split and two national scores. Does not get the six-item advocacy agenda, the evidence per item, or the ask.

Regional DMO board3/5

Ten packages and first-wave five with targets are there. The 18-activity table with owners and success criteria is not.

Mayors2/5

Can find their district's deltas. Cannot find the study's "what to do first" per district, the parking value, or any download.

What is right

The results agent verified every figure it could find against the study and reported no numeric discrepancies. The visual agent found no layout breakage.

  • All 24 group scores in the "Nézőpontok" cards
  • Seven district indices and all 56 district sub-index values
  • The eight weakest and eight strongest questions, with N
  • Sample counts 632 / 246 / 170 / 1048, and 34 municipal
  • Thresholds 15 / 10, change thresholds 1 / 2 / 5
  • Weighting sensitivity 63,4 vs 64,0
  • The three municipal-vs-resident pairs 82/47, 69/50, 64/45
  • Canonical, hreflang, HTTPS, www redirect all correct
  • No horizontal overflow, no broken images, no overlaps
  • Colour palette survives colour-blindness; legends present
  • Loads in about 1.2 s, four requests total
  • English page fully translated, right register
  • "Not a ranking, not a fund-allocation key" caveats present

Unfinished parts

  • Dead links Five footer links point to "#": the full study PDF, the executive summary PDF, Sajtókapcsolat, Impresszum, Adatkezelési tájékoztató. Same on the English page.
  • Placeholder The thank-you section shows the literal text [partnerek felsorolása / logósor — egyeztetés után].
  • Share image Both pages reference og-2026.png, which returns 404. Shares on Facebook and LinkedIn show no image.
  • Script-only numbers The hero counts, all sub-index and district values, and the whole FAQ are inserted by JavaScript. Visitors see them. Print, screen readers, search snippets and anyone copying text get a dash.

Framing the study warns against

The study's section 6.10.1 names four typical misreadings. The site guards against two and commits two.

MisreadingSiteEvidence
Ranking districtsHalf guardedThe caveat paragraph is good. But the district list is sorted by score with one decimal, the map is coloured by value, Fonyód (+2,3) sits in the top band with Keszthely and Füred, and Veszprém is coloured although the study says its result is not comparable.
Index as a targetCommittedKözös mérce, hogy a Balaton évről évre jobb hely legyen. The study's point that fixing all 15 weak questions moves the index only 1,3 points never appears, so "41% of the fixable gap" has no denominator.
Fund allocation keyGuardednem forráselosztási kulcs
Single number as verdictCommittedHero and results lede quoted above. The study asks for the structure instead: attachment 84, governance 47, no guest question below the midpoint, 35-point gap between municipal self-view and resident view.

One more caveat the study is careful about and the site drops: guests were reached in Tourinform offices and at accommodation, mid-stay and in season, and were not asked any governance, liveability or competitiveness questions. "A vendég elégedett" is true within that scope. The site states it without the scope.

What each audience cannot find

Journalists and the public

  • Publication date, fieldwork dates beyond "2026 nyarán", a named press contact with a phone number.
  • Any downloadable asset: data table, chart images, press kit, English summary PDF.
  • The eight sub-index values and seven district values as plain text. Only 84,4 appears in copy; 46,7 appears nowhere in copy.
  • For a Siófok or Tapolca journalist: why their district is low. The study has it (Siófok providers rate attractiveness at 43,9, 22 below the region; Tapolca residents feel benefits at 44,1, 20 below), the site does not.

National tourism board

  • The six-item national agenda of the study's table 6.9: water management, investment incentives, administrative burden, shoreline construction and public access, seasonal employment, transport links, each with evidence and the requested initiative. Only two of the six are mentioned on the site, and none as an ask.
  • Table 5.22: the 15 weak questions with leverage, district spread and competence level. This is the study's actionable core.
  • Authors' names, a citation string, version and date, the 82-question code table, the questionnaires.
  • Who submits and who co-signs: the Balatoni Fejlesztési Tanács and the kerekasztal are not named on the site.

Mayors and the DMO board

  • Table 6.8: each district's weakest point and "mit érdemes elsőként". The cards give deltas only.
  • District parking values (Fonyód 23,3 to Marcali 47,8), the Veszprém "two resident answers" warning.
  • The 18-activity program with owner, indicator, success criterion and resource type, and the DMO's seven self-measures.
  • A sample composition box: per-district N, resident segments, online vs paper, guest language share. The study says this must be published every year.

Mobile and visual

  • High Tapping a district in the list updates a card 1,200 px further down without scrolling to it. Map taps do scroll. The list tap feels like nothing happened.
  • High The methodology flow diagram scales to 312 px wide on phones; its labels render at 4 to 5 px.
  • Medium The sticky header covers section headings after nav jumps. Needs scroll-margin-top on the section targets.
  • Medium Map district labels are 8 px. Muted captions at 12 to 13 px on #6b8089 fail AA contrast; the ochre eyebrow on the ochre card is 3.2:1.
  • Low Google Fonts stylesheet blocks first render. Self-hosting the two Manrope files removes the round-trip.
